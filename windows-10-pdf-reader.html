How to Set Nighthawk as Your Default PDF Reader in Windows 10

Tired of Microsoft Edge hijacking your PDFs? Here is how to permanently set Nighthawk as your default viewer:

  1. Press the Windows Key and type Default Apps, then press Enter.
  2. Scroll down and click the link that says Choose default apps by file type.
  3. Scroll down the alphabetical list on the left until you find .pdf.
  4. Click the application listed next to it (usually Microsoft Edge or a grey plus icon).
  5. Select Nighthawk PDF Reader from the list. Your PDFs will now always open instantly in Nighthawk.
