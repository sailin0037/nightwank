Zero Telemetry. No Cloud Uploads. Complete Privacy.

In modern digital environments, user data is constantly monetized and uploaded to cloud servers. Most PDF readers have transitioned into "cloud services," tracking when you open a document, how long you read it, and which features you use. Nighthawk PDF Reader is engineered with a completely different philosophy.

The Air-Gapped Guarantee

Nighthawk remains strictly offline. It respects your privacy, meaning what happens on your PC stays on your PC. There are no background sync processes, no mandatory accounts, and no hidden data harvesting. The application does not even contain the network socket libraries required to transmit telemetry data.

Why Your Data Matters

When handling massive documents—ranging from complex architectural blueprints to thousands of pages of legal discovery or sensitive patient records—the bottleneck in security is often the software viewing it. By processing everything locally, Nighthawk ensures compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and stringent corporate confidentiality policies.

Looking for Advanced Protection?

For users handling highly sensitive files, Nighthawk features an encrypted Biometric Secure Vault integrated directly with Windows Hello, allowing you to lock documents behind your fingerprint or Face ID.
