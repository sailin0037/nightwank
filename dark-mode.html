How to Enable Dark Mode in PDF Reader

Staring at bright white PDF documents late at night can cause severe eye strain. While many PDF readers offer a basic UI dark theme, they leave the document itself blindingly white. Here is how to enable true, biological dark mode for your PDFs.

The Nighthawk Solution

Nighthawk PDF Reader was built from the ground up for night reading. Instead of just inverting colors (which destroys images and readability), Nighthawk uses custom HLSL shaders to intelligently map document colors to eye-friendly palettes.

Available Biological Modes:

  • Predator Mode: Deep crimson and black. Preserves night vision adaptation completely.
  • Night Vision: Tactical green tones, offering higher contrast while maintaining low light emission.
  • Sepia: Warm, comforting tones simulating old paper, perfect for reducing blue light exposure before sleep.

Steps to Enable Dark Mode

  1. Download Nighthawk PDF Reader (it's completely free and lightweight).
  2. Open any PDF document.
  3. Click the Mode Picker icon in the top right corner.
  4. Select your preferred biological dark mode. The document will instantly transform via hardware-accelerated shaders.
